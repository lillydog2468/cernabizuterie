Traditional Jablonec glass jewellery since 1882

Černá bižuterie

Black jewellery has been an important part of Jablonec industry since 1882. Its popularity peaked around the death of Queen Victoria (1901), when this ornament became mourning jewellery for the countries of the British Empire. This glass jewellery is made in Jablonec nad Nisou from the original material.
